## Trauma Cards - Dreamscaper Inc.

### Core Mechanic
Each character has a personal Trauma Deck.
Cards represent fears, past events, defense mechanisms.

Playing cards resolves client dreams but risks personal breakdown.

### Example Cards (v0.1)

#### 1. Fear of Failure
- **Cost**: 2 Lucidity
- **Effect**: Deal heavy damage to boss, but next failure causes extra stress.
- **Art**: Broken ladder, falling figure (see concept art)

#### 2. Abandonment Issues
- **Cost**: 1 Lucidity
- **Effect**: Summon ally spirit, but if ally 'dies' in dream, player takes permanent scar.
- **Art**: Reaching for fading figure, broken chains

#### 3. Impostor Syndrome
- **Cost**: 0
- **Effect**: Copy enemy's ability, but your cards cost more next turn.

#### 4. Childhood Trauma (Rare)
- **Cost**: 3 Lucidity
- **Effect**: Massive area heal + damage, but unlocks hidden boss phase.
