## More Trauma Card Examples

### Abandonment (Loss)
- Power: 2
- Effect: Creates "Echo" enemies that chase the player. Increases loneliness debuff.

### Perfectionism (Control)
- Power: 4
- Effect: Cards are stronger but cost extra "Focus". Risk of overthinking collapse.

### Childhood Innocence (Nostalgia)
- Power: 1
- Effect: Temporary safe zone + heal. But reveals hidden past dangers.

### Betrayal (Trust)
- Power: 3
- Effect: Random ally betrayal event or powerful single-target attack.

### Existential Dread (Void)
- Power: 5
- Effect: Massive area damage + high instability. "The end is near" flavor.

Add these as TraumaCard resources in Godot.
