## Example Trauma Cards

### Fear of Failure
- Type: Fear
- Power: 3
- Effect: When played, risk of dream collapse increases, but high reward damage

### Abandonment
- Type: Loss
- Power: 2
- Effect: Summons "echoes" that follow the player

### Perfectionism
- Type: Control
- Power: 4
- Effect: Cards cost more but are stronger

### Childhood Memory
- Type: Nostalgia
- Power: 1
- Effect: Heals but reveals hidden dangers
