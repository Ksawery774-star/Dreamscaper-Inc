## Art Direction - Dreamscaper Inc.

### Visual Style
- Surreal dream aesthetics mixed with corporate horror
- Color palette: Deep blues, purples, neon accents, decaying pastels
- 2.5D or pixel-art hybrid
- Strong contrast between safe hub and chaotic dreams

### Key Visuals
- Trauma cards with personal, hand-drawn feel
- Dream worlds that morph based on player traumas

### Reference Games
- Psychonauts
- Control
- Hades (card art style)
- Dreamscaper (original game for inspiration, but we go deeper)
